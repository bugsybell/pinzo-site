Your golf,
beautifully kept.

Every course you've played, every round and every score, and the places you still dream of, in one beautiful passport.

Coming soon to the App Store Free on iPhone

Golf takes you to extraordinary places: a links at first light, a clifftop par three, a trip abroad with old friends. Pinzo keeps them all. Every course, every country and every round worth remembering, in one beautiful passport, with the places you're still dreaming of waiting on the next page.

01

Remember everywhere you've played.

Add a lifetime of golf in minutes. Search more than 31,000 courses, tick the ones you remember, and add dates and scores only if you want to. Rounds recorded on Apple Watch come in from Apple Health.

02

A stamp for every country.

Play somewhere new and Pinzo presses a stamp into your passport. Fifty milestone medals arrive too, in bronze, silver and gold: your fiftieth course, breaking eighty, the Old Course, a golf trip abroad.

03

Dream about what's next.

Fly over any course in 3D, find the ones near you, and keep a bucket list of places you've always wanted to play. Pinzo marks the day you finally do.

Every round, a memory.

Add photos of the day, a note and a scan of your scorecard to any round. Years later, Pinzo brings it back on the day: “3 years ago today, you played Royal County Down.” Finish a round with your Apple Watch and it can remind you to log it.

Golf with friends, privately.

Invite the people you play with and see where each other has been: their courses, their stamps, and a note when they play somewhere new. Friends are added with a private link, so nobody can find you unless you ask them to. Photos and notes stay yours, and scores stay private unless you choose to share them.

Made to be shared.

Every round, stamp and milestone becomes a postcard for your Stories, designed and made on your iPhone.

Every course in the world.

Championship links, nine-hole gems, par-three courses and indoor simulators in over 180 countries. Can't find yours? Add it in seconds.

Private by design.

No ads, no tracking and no account required. Your passport lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, where we can't see it. Friends only ever see what you choose to share. Read our privacy policy.
