Questions

My course isn't in Pinzo

In Log a round, search for it and tap Can't find it? Add it (or search in Explore). Give it a name, choose the country and drop the pin. It's private to you and counts towards your passport straight away. Missing courses are often added to the main list in later updates, too.

How do indoor rounds count?

Choose Indoor golf when logging. Indoor venues count towards your countries and stamps, and you can note the virtual course you played — but a simulator round at “Pebble Beach” doesn't count as playing Pebble Beach.

Can I add rounds from years ago?

Yes. Tap Log → Past rounds and add every course you remember in one go. Dates and scores are optional — choose an exact date, month, year or “I don't remember”, and add details later from any round.

Can I import rounds from Hole19, 18Birdies or Golfshot?

If they save your rounds to Apple Health (most do, as do Apple Watch golf workouts), tap Log → Apple Health. Pinzo reads your golf workouts on your iPhone and suggests the course for each one. Those apps don't offer a direct connection to other apps.

What are milestones?

Alongside a stamp for every country, Pinzo awards milestone seals — courses played, rounds logged, breaking 100, 90 and 80, the Old Course, Open venues and more. Find them in your passport; tap one to see your progress or share it.

Which notifications does Pinzo send?

Only the ones you choose: a reminder to log after Apple Health records a golf round, occasional “on this day” memories and new stamps (all created on your iPhone), and, if you use Friends, a note when a friend plays.

Do I need an account?

No. Your passport lives on your iPhone and in your iCloud. You only sign in with Apple if you want to use Friends.

Will I lose my passport if I change phone?

Not if iCloud is on for Pinzo. Your passport syncs privately through your iCloud, so it appears when you sign in to the same Apple Account on your new iPhone.

Is my data shared with anyone?

Only with friends you add yourself, and only where and when you played (plus scores if you choose). There are no ads and no tracking, and photos and notes are never shared. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete everything?

Profile → Settings → Delete all my data. It removes everything from your device and your iCloud and can't be undone.

Some course details are wrong

Course information comes from OpenStreetMap, which anyone can improve at openstreetmap.org — corrections flow into Pinzo in later updates. Or email us and we'll take a look.

How do I add friends?

Go to your Profile and tap Invite a friend. Pinzo makes a private link that works once; send it by Messages, WhatsApp or anything else. When your friend taps it and accepts, you'll see each other's courses, countries, stamps and new rounds. There's no search, so nobody can find you unless you send them a link.

Can I add who I played with?

Yes. When you log a round, tap friends under Played with, or type the name of anyone who isn't on Pinzo. Friends you tag get a note and can add the round to their own passport with one tap. Names you type stay on your iPhone.

Can friends see my scores, photos or notes?

Photos and notes are never shared. Scores stay private unless you turn on Share my scores in Settings → Friends.

How do I remove, block or report someone?

Open their profile and tap ⋯ in the corner. Removing or blocking is instant and they aren't told. Reports come to us and we look at them within 48 hours.

I've got a new iPhone. Where are my friends?

Your passport comes back by itself through iCloud. For Friends, go to Profile or Settings and tap Already use Friends? Sign in, then continue with the same Apple Account. Your friends reappear straight away. You can sign out of Friends at any time in Settings; your account and friends are kept.

Can I hide the Friends invitation?

Yes. Tap the × on it. You can still invite friends later from Settings → Friends.

My invite link says it has expired

Each link works once and lasts seven days. Ask your friend to send a new one from their Profile.
