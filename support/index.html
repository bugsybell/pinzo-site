Questions

My course isn't in Pinzo

In Log a round, search for it and tap Can't find it? Add it (or search in Explore). Give it a name, choose the country and drop the pin. It's private to you and counts towards your passport straight away. Missing courses are often added to the main list in later updates, too.

How do indoor rounds count?

Choose Indoor golf when logging. Indoor venues count towards your countries and stamps, and you can note the virtual course you played — but a simulator round at “Pebble Beach” doesn't count as playing Pebble Beach.

Can I add rounds from years ago?

Yes. Tap Log → Past rounds and add every course you remember in one go. Dates and scores are optional — choose an exact date, month, year or “I don't remember”, and add details later from any round.

Can I import rounds from Hole19, 18Birdies or Golfshot?

If they save your rounds to Apple Health (most do, as do Apple Watch golf workouts), tap Log → Apple Health. Pinzo reads your golf workouts on your iPhone and suggests the course for each one. Those apps don't offer a direct connection to other apps.

What are milestones?

Alongside a stamp for every country, Pinzo awards milestone seals — courses played, rounds logged, breaking 100, 90 and 80, the Old Course, Open venues and more. Find them in your passport; tap one to see your progress or share it.

Which notifications does Pinzo send?

Only the ones you choose in Settings → Notifications: a reminder to log after Apple Health records a golf round, occasional “on this day” memories, and new stamps. All are created on your iPhone.

Do I need an account?

No. Your passport lives on your iPhone and in your iCloud. An account is optional and only reserves a username for when friends arrive.

Will I lose my passport if I change phone?

Not if iCloud is on for Pinzo. Your passport syncs privately through your iCloud, so it appears when you sign in to the same Apple Account on your new iPhone.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and we can't see your passport. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete everything?

Profile → Settings → Delete all my data. It removes everything from your device and your iCloud and can't be undone.

Some course details are wrong

Course information comes from OpenStreetMap, which anyone can improve at openstreetmap.org — corrections flow into Pinzo in later updates. Or email us and we'll take a look.

Can I add friends?

Not yet — but you can claim your username now in Settings. Meanwhile, share a round or your whole passport as an image with the share button.
