Your content

The rounds, photos, notes and venues you add belong to you and stay in your device and iCloud. We don't claim any rights over them and can't access them. Please only add photos you have the right to use.

Friends

Friends is optional and is for people aged 16 and over. When you use it, your name, home town, handicap, favourite course and where and when you played (and your scores, if you choose) are visible to the friends you add. Please:

  • use your real name or a name your friends know you by, and nothing offensive or misleading;
  • don't pretend to be someone else, or log rounds to mislead or harass anyone;
  • only send invite links to people you know.

You can remove, block or report a friend at any time from their profile. We review reports and may suspend or delete accounts that break these rules. You can leave Friends and delete your account at any time in Settings.
